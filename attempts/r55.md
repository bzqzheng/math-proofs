# Attempt: R(5,5) ≥ 44 via circulant witness (Tier-1 #7)

## Status: SCAN RUNNING, 2026-07-24

## Calibration
43 ≤ R(5,5) ≤ 46 (lower: Exoo 1989, a (5,5)-graph on 42 vertices; upper:
Angeltveit–McKay, SAT-based). A (5,5)-graph on 43 vertices — no K5, no
independent 5-set — improves a 37-year-old lower bound. Any such graph is
a machine-checkable witness (verification = this script's oracle).

## Approach (lottery, honestly priced)
43 is prime; circulants C_43(S), S ⊆ {1..21} distances. WLOG 1 ∈ S
(multiply by d^{-1} — isomorphism), so 2^20 ≈ 1.05M candidates; complement
of a circulant is a circulant, so checking ω ≤ 4 AND α ≤ 4 per candidate
covers the space. Oracle: bitset backtracking, cliques/independent sets
through vertex 0 (rotational symmetry). Smoke: 10k sets/s → full space
~2 minutes.

## Prior
~zero. Circulant spaces on Ramsey-critical sizes were trawled decades ago
(Exoo, McKay et al.); if a 43-circulant existed it would be the bound.
This run is due diligence + reusable tooling (the checker generalizes to
other witness hunts: Folkman numbers, other (s,t)-graphs). A null result
closes the circulant lane permanently and points any real attempt at
SAT/local-search over general graphs — a different, much heavier project.

## General graphs lane (not started)
Exoo-style simulated annealing on edge flips with the (5,5)-property as
objective is the published method; a meaningful attempt is weeks of CPU.
Not scheduled this iteration.
